Schem das Américas.

A company founded in 2015 to take an active role in the distribution of raw materials for animal and plant nutrition — today also in steel & foundry.

Mission

To develop and sell chemical products for our clients across various agribusiness segments, adding value through our knowledge and services delivered by committed, skilled professionals, aiming for satisfaction and stronger partnerships with stakeholders and sustainable brand consolidation in the market.

Vision

To become a reference company in solution innovation for our stakeholders in the chemical products market.

Our history

From São Paulo to Brazil and the world.

Founded in 2015, Schem das Américas quickly became a reference in the national and international distribution of raw materials focused on agriculture, animal and plant nutrition. Today we're among the best-positioned distributors in agribusiness — and we've expanded into Steel & Foundry, exporting to North America, South America and Europe.
